## RDF model

RDF describes resources through subject–predicate–object triples. Graphs combine triples; IRIs identify resources, literals represent values, and blank nodes represent unnamed resources. The primer introduces RDF 1.2 triple terms and multiple graphs.

## Formats and queries

The primer surveys Turtle, TriG, N-Triples, N-Quads, JSON-LD, RDFa and RDF/XML. SPARQL queries graph relationships independently of their serialization. Consult the linked normative specifications for implementation conformance.
